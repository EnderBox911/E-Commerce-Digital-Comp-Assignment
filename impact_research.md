# Shopee – Impact and Current Market Research

## 1. Consumer Impact

Shopee changed the way people shop in Southeast Asia. Search for products, compare prices, use of vouchers and phone payments.

It is commonplace now that people use online shopping as their daily activity. Google says that 3 out of 5 people in Southeast Asia are online shoppers (2025), (Bain & Company, 2025). The report also revealed that over 60% of payments are digital.

### Impact

The consumer can easily and conveniently shop without visiting physical shops.

### Why This Demonstrates Disruption

Shopping is no longer confined to brick and mortar locations, it has gone online and mobile. Now consumers are able to find, compare and purchase products on their phones.

Google, Temasek, & Bain & Company (2025), *e-Conomy SEA 2025*

## 2. Impact on Sellers and SMEs


Shopee facilitated local sellers and small businesses to sell online. The seller doesn't have to develop his own website. They are able to add products, interact with customers and utilize Shopee's payment and logistics services.

Shopee Rai Lokal helped over 1.8 million local entrepreneurs in 2024 in Malaysia.

### Impact

Small businesses can get into the online marketplace with much less hassle than big ones, and target their customers beyond the boundaries of their community.

### Why This Demonstrates Disruption

Typical small business was typically reliant on physical stores, markets or neighborhood customers. Internet marketplaces provide them with a different avenue to peddle products and expand their reach.

## 3. Impact on Existing Market

Shopee brought a heady dose of competition into the Southeast Asian e-commerce market. Competition from online platforms and sellers is achieved by price, vouchers, delivery speed, product variety and services available online.

Momentum Works (2025) predicts that Southeast Asia's platform e-commerce GMV will rise to US$128.4 billion in 2024. Shopee accounted for approximately 52% of the regional market share while the combined market share of Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada accounted for more than 84%.

### Impact

The competition increased and digital platforms took more and more ground in the market.

### Why This Demonstrates Disruption

Large online marketplaces pose a threat to traditional retailers and other e-commerce sites. There is also a need to enhance the businesses online services, their prices and their delivery.

### Source

Momentum Works (2025), *Ecommerce in Southeast Asia 3.0*

## 4. Continuing Disruption

It wasn't just regular online shopping that was upended. Social commerce, livestreaming and video shopping are all driving the growth of e-commerce.

Shopee offers some tools like Shopee Live and Shopee Video that enable sellers to broadcast the product and communicate with customers online.

According to Google, Temasek and Bain (2025), about 25% of e-commerce GMV in Southeast Asia was from video commerce in 2025.

### Impact

Consumers can find products using videos and livestreams in addition to searching for products.

### How This Demonstrates Disruption

Shopping is transforming from searching for products online to entertaining, social media and shopping.

## 5. Current Market Competition

Shopee is still one of the leading e-commerce platforms in Southeast Asia, though the competition is continuously evolving.

In 2025, Southeast Asia's ecommerce GMV was estimated to be US$157.6 billion by Momentum Works (2026). Shopee accounted for approximately 53% of the market share in the region. TikTok Shop, including Tokopedia, also kept on expanding.

### Impact

With other platforms also vying with Shopee for consumers and sellers' attention, the company must strive to improve its services.

### Why This Demonstrates Disruption

The business of e-commerce is not just about putting products on the web. Platforms now integrate shopping and social media, video, content and livestreaming.
